# Music source — pilot episode 01

`music.wav` is an original, locally synthesized, quiet harmonic pad generated by `generate-audio.mjs`. It uses simple sine-wave notes and a slow fade; no sampled melody, vocals, third-party recording, or external music file is used.

The final mux applies automatic sidechain ducking from the combined dialogue to the music. Dialogue stays at the front; the music bed reduces while anyone speaks and returns gently in pauses.
